## Deborah Beckett

GRC operator working in the gap between how fast a business ships and how fast its compliance matures.

I own customer trust at WordPress VIP, the enterprise WordPress hosting business within Automattic. That means SOC 2, the security questionnaire program, third-party risk management, and the evidence underneath all of it.

Both repos here came out of that job. The SOC 2 guide is what I learned taking a program through its first audit cycle. The questionnaire toolkit is what I built because answering them by hand didn't scale.

**[soc2-verdancy](https://github.com/beckettgrc/soc2-verdancy)** – Where to put your attention before your first SOC 2 observation period opens. Sorts your control list by two questions nobody asks, and makes the case that the wording of a control is a choice you still get to make.

**[ddq-toolkit](https://github.com/beckettgrc/ddq-toolkit)** – A Claude skill for resolving portal-based security questionnaires, built on a validated answer bank rather than on-the-fly generation. It uses approved answers verbatim where it can and flags where it's synthesizing, so a human reviews the judgment calls instead of the whole document.
